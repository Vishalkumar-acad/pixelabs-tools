💡 About Us

Who we are, why this site exists, and why you can trust it with your files.

What is PixelAbs Tools?

PixelAbs Tools is a growing suite of 30+ free online utilities — image compression, PDF merge & split, audio trimming, QR codes, developer helpers and more. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser: there is no sign-up, no ads, no file uploads and no tracking of any kind.

We built it because most "free online tools" make you pay with your data. This one doesn't. Your files are processed by your own device, so they never leave your hands — you can even disconnect from the internet and most tools keep working.

Privacy first, by design

Every tool defaults to local processing. A few heavy operations (like large video-to-GIF conversions) optionally offer a cloud mode — it is clearly labelled, off by default, and warns you before your file is sent anywhere. Files sent to our free processing server are deleted immediately after processing. Nothing is ever stored.

We don't use cookies for accounts (there are no accounts), no analytics scripts, and no third-party CDNs — even the fonts and libraries are self-hosted. The only data we ever collect is anonymous error messages, so we can fix bugs.

Open source

The entire project is open source under the MIT license. You can read every line of code, verify our privacy claims yourself, and even run your own copy:

🔗 github.com/Vishalkumar-acad/pixelabs-tools

Self-hosting takes about two minutes — clone the repo, run one build script, and deploy it free on Cloudflare Pages. Full instructions are in the README.

How it's built

No frameworks, no bundlers, no build drama — just plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, because fast and simple beats fancy. The site is a Progressive Web App (PWA), so it installs on your phone and works offline. All third-party libraries are pinned versions, checksum-verified and served from our own origin.
