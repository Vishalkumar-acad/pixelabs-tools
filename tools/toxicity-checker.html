🛡️ Toxicity Checker

Paste a comment or post and see how abusive it reads — with the raw score, not just a yes or no.

This tool is different. Every other tool here runs entirely on your device. Checking toxicity needs a 64 MB language model, which cannot run on every phone — so this one sends the text you paste to our own server, where the model runs. It is not stored or logged, and it is discarded as soon as the score comes back.

Up to 5,000 characters. Tip: Ctrl/⌘ + Enter to check.

This is a reading of the text, not a verdict. Very short messages, sarcasm, reclaimed words and languages the model never saw can all be wrong in both directions — treat it as one opinion, and let a human make the call.

How the score is decided

1. Abusive wordsCommon English and Hindi slurs and swearing are caught outright, even when written as sh1t or f u c k.
2. Harassment phrasesPolitely-worded attacks such as "nobody likes you" are matched as phrases.
3. The modelEverything else is scored by a DistilBERT classifier fine-tuned on 1.75M public comments. Clean text usually lands near 0.000, clearly abusive text near 0.95.
